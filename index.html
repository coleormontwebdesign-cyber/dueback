Straight answers

Wouldn't calling them myself work better?

Calls work. Mail reaches the ones whose numbers changed or who moved. Best is both: we can hand your office a street-sorted call list and a short script to go with the cards.

How do I know they called because of the card? I get a lot of repeat customers.

A booking counts when a home we mailed books within 90 days, or when a customer mentions the card or scans its code. Jobs already on your schedule before the cards land don't count. About 10% of your list isn't mailed, so you can see how many of those book on their own. You get one invoice a month listing every address.

Where does the tank size and location come from?

Your own records: QuickBooks notes, your field app, or paper tickets. If you don't have tank size, the card shows the last pump-out date and skips that line.

Do I have to offer a discount?

Your call. Some options: a neighbor deal (book with a neighbor the same week and your truck does two tanks in one trip), or a book-by date.

How many of these have you run?

Straight answer: DueBack is new. Our first pumper's cards go out in November 2026. That's why you pay nothing for cards that don't book. Your only cost before a truck rolls is printing and postage, and you approve that quote first. Start small, with your first 100 to 200 homes.

Is my customer list safe?

Your list is used only for your mailings. It's never shared or sold, and it's deleted when you ask. I'll sign a one-page confidentiality agreement. For the count, you can send just the town and last pump-out date. Never send card or bank details.
