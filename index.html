Straight answers

Wouldn't calling them myself work better?

Calls work. Mail reaches the ones whose numbers changed or who moved. Best is both: we can hand your office a street-sorted call list and a short script to go with the cards.

How do I know they called because of the card? I get a lot of repeat customers.

Each mailing has its own tracking number that forwards to your office. About 10% of the list is held back and not mailed, so you can compare. You only pay for bookings that come in on the card's number.

Where does the tank size and location come from?

Your own records: QuickBooks notes, your field app, or paper tickets. If you don't have tank size, the card shows the last pump-out date and skips that line.

Do I have to offer a discount?

Your call. Some options: a neighbor deal (book with a neighbor the same week and your truck does two tanks in one trip), or a book-by date.

How many of these have you run?

Straight answer: DueBack is new. We're working with founding pumpers now. That's why our fee is $0 until a truck rolls. You carry no risk beyond postage, and you can start small, around 200 cards.

Is my customer list safe?

Your list is never shared or sold, and it's deleted when you ask. For the count, you can send just town and last pump-out date.

What does it cost?

Nothing up front and no monthly fee. You pay only per tank actually pumped from the campaign, plus postage at cost. The per-tank rate is set with each founding pumper.
